# Glossary

- **Cell**: one addressable square in the editable grid.
- **Selected cell**: the single cell chosen by the user as the only audio-reactive target.
- **Audio preset**: a named configuration that maps an audio reaction to the selected cell's scale.
- **Reaction**: a live signal derived from audio, such as level, kick, bass, or BPM.
- **Shaped signal**: the reaction after threshold, attack/release, sensitivity, and optional step quantisation.
- **Reactive scale**: the selected cell's scale after mapping the shaped signal into the configured range.
